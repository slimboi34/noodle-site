Noodle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Noodle is built to know as little about you as possible. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your first name (if you choose to enter one), your goals, game levels, scores, streaks, badges and reminder time are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app, or using Me › Erase my progress, deletes them.

Purchases

If you buy Noodle Pro, Apple processes the payment and the app checks your Pro status directly with the App Store on your device. No purchase information is sent to us or to anyone else.

Game Center

Signing in to Apple Game Center is optional. If you do, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile so they can appear on leaderboards, and Noodle shows your Game Center nickname and your friends' nicknames and ranks. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive or store it. You can sign out in iOS Settings › Game Center.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved and we cannot see whether you enabled them.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no IDFA.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.

Children

Noodle is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Invite, iOS shows its standard share sheet. Only the text you see is shared, and only with the app and people you pick.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be posted here. Questions? See Support.
