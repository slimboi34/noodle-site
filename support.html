Help & Support

Contact us

Opens a support ticket. We usually reply within two working days.

I bought Pro on another iPhone. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Me › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Noodle. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your phone. There's no account, so progress doesn't move between devices yet. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Why did my level go down?

Every game adapts after each round. Score 85% or better and it levels up; below 60% it eases off. That keeps every round a fair challenge.

My streak disappeared

A streak continues while you finish at least one game each day. Streak freezes (you earn one every 7 days, up to 2) cover a single missed day automatically.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check Settings › Notifications › Noodle is allowed, then toggle the reminder off and on in Me.

Can I suggest a game?

Please do. Use the contact button above. New games ship in free updates.
