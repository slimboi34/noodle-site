Honest about the science

Noodle's games are inspired by well-studied tasks such as n-back, Stroop, flanker, trail making and mental rotation. Practice makes you better at the games, and they are a fun way to keep your mind busy. We don't claim Noodle will raise your IQ, prevent memory loss or treat any condition, and nobody honestly can.
